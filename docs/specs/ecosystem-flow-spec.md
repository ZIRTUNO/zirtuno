# S3 — Connections

Owner-authorized full rebuild, September 2026. This replaces the accumulating
Gathering column and its static mobile list.

## Direction

Three business relationships become one physical structure: identity gives a
business a recognizable presence; growth carries that presence into a
relationship; operations carry the relationship forward. Four short, authored
statements explain that sequence in PT-BR and English.

The sculpture uses the site's own cyan liquid. Three open currents close into
tilted loops, touch, and resolve into the existing Confluence footprint. Its
negative spaces make connection legible. Slow deformation continues while the
reader pauses. The field's existing hand and strike interaction remains live.

References informed technique, not visual identity:

- [UpSunday](https://www.upsunday.co/): confident scale and one clear visual
  focus per screen, with graphics composed into the editorial rhythm.
- [GSAP scrubbed bento](https://demos.gsap.com/demo/scrubbed-bento-gallery/):
  reversible progress and geometric transitions tied to a single scroll clock.
- [React Bits Scroll Mask](https://pro.reactbits.dev/docs/components/scroll-mask)
  and [Liquid Swap](https://pro.reactbits.dev/docs/components/liquid-swap):
  complementary masks and continuity across changing states. No paid source,
  component or dependency was copied or installed.

## Ownership

- `ChapterEcosystem.tsx`: server-rendered meaning, native details, semantic
  chapter controls and the existing structure CTA.
- `ecosystem.css`: normal-flow baseline, sticky composition and type masks.
- `ecosystem-score.ts`: paused GSAP timeline sampled by PageStage's existing
  measurement loop. It writes CSS variables and active/inert state, and routes
  chapter control clicks through the existing Lenis instance.
- `ecosystem-flow.mjs`: allocation-free 48-droplet target function. At progress
  one, positions and radii equal the Confluence source exactly.
- `scenes/site.ts`: maps those targets into the existing scene, conductor and
  Services bridge. The three currents each carry 16 canonical droplets.
- `FieldStage.tsx`: optional scene gloss gain, default zero. S3 supplies 0.72;
  the gain returns to zero during its exit. Shader source and exact resting
  form optics are unchanged. No extra canvas or animation loop is introduced.

## Responsive and accessible behavior

Wide screens pair left-aligned copy with a large sculpture on the right.
Below 1024px, the sculpture occupies the upper half and copy the lower half.
Short portrait screens tighten the type scale. Viewports under 640px high use
the illustrated normal-flow document so no reading step is trapped in a pin.

The three chapter buttons remain visible during the live sequence. Native
`details` expose every capability explanation by touch, pointer and keyboard.
One live panel is accessible at a time; leaving a focused panel moves focus to
the corresponding chapter control before making that panel inert. Scroll in
either direction restores the corresponding content. Closed explanations are
native disclosures, not hover-only tooltips.

Reduced motion, no JavaScript and static tiers preserve all copy, all ten
disclosures and the CTA in document order. The Confluence SVG is present in
server HTML. In the live composition it hides only when the shared liquid
reports a drawn frame; a context loss restores it.

## Verification

`scripts/verify/ecosystem.mjs` covers finite geometry, exact bridge endpoint,
forward/reverse reading, desktop/phone/tablet containment, native disclosure
input, navigation, persistent canvas, idle rendering, context loss and the
four static reading paths. Screenshots and measurements go to
`captures/ecosystem/`. The capture harness produces four states at 1440×900
and 390×844 in PT-BR, plus the English phone sequence.

Run the conductor, boundaries, accessibility, device, canvas count and
rest-exact gates alongside the normal TypeScript, lint and build checks.
Emulation cannot establish real iOS toolbar behavior, thermal performance or
the owner's final judgment of the motion.
